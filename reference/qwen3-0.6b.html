Facts worth keeping

  • Row t of the logits predicts token t+1 from tokens 0..t.
  • With all 28 blocks removed, the model predicts its own input token, at 271 of 272 positions measured. Its logits reach about 116, past the fp32 exp limit.
  • fp32 overflows above about e88.7 ≈ 3.4 × 10³⁸. Softmax is shift-invariant, so subtract the row max first.
  • ε goes inside the square root.
  • The embedding is about 156M of the 0.6B parameters. The model card gives 0.44B non-embedding.
