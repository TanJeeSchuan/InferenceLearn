Why keepdim=True

For x of shape (2, 3), x.sum(-1) returns (2,): one total per row. Dividing (2, 3) by (2,) lines the shapes up from the right, so the 2 totals get matched against the 3 columns, which is an error or, worse, the wrong answer. x.sum(-1, keepdim=True) returns (2, 1), and dividing (2, 3) by (2, 1) stretches each row's single total across its 3 columns. That's what you want: every element divided by its own row's total.

Worked: softmax

softmax(z)i = exp(zi) / Σj exp(zj)

Read it as: "the i-th output is exp of the i-th input, divided by the sum of exp over all inputs."

The two columns end the same way, which is the shift-invariance argument in numbers. Subtracting 2 shrank every exp by the same factor e² ≈ 7.39, and that factor cancels in the division. The right column can't overflow, because its largest exponent is 0.
